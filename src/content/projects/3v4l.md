3v4l (EvalKit) is an LLM evaluation and observability harness — a
system for measuring how well your language models are actually
performing on your data, with the metrics flowing into a live
Grafana dashboard.

The eval suite runs structured test cases against any OpenAI-compatible
endpoint, scoring responses on hallucination rate, factual accuracy,
and latency. Each query produces a reasoning trace with per-step
metrics that flow into Prometheus, so you can watch model quality
degrade or improve as you swap backends, change prompts, or fine-tune.

The Grafana dashboard shows live eval metrics: per-model accuracy
distributions, hallucination rates over time, latency percentiles,
and per-query drilldowns. This turns model quality from a subjective
judgment into a measurable, trackable signal.
